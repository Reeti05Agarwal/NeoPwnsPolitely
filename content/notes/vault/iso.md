## **ISO/IEC 27001 (International Organization for Standardization)**

### Overview:

The **ISO/IEC 27001** standard outlines requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS). It is internationally recognized and focuses on securing data confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

### Key Features:

1. **Risk-Based Approach:**
    
    - Identifies risks and applies controls based on their severity.
    - Encourages a lifecycle of continuous improvement.
2. **Annex A Controls (ISO/IEC 27002):**
    
    - Provides a set of security controls (e.g., access control, cryptography, incident management).
3. **Certification:**
    
    - Organizations can be certified by accredited bodies.

### Application:

- Suitable for any organization seeking to formalize its information security processes.
- Often required for partnerships, especially in international trade.

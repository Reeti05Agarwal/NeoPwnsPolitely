## **Risk Management Framework (RMF)**

### Overview:

The **Risk Management Framework (RMF)** is a structured process developed by NIST to integrate information security and risk management into federal systems.

### Key Features:

1. **Seven Steps of RMF:**
    
    - **Prepare:** Establish a risk management program.
    - **Categorize:** Define the system and assign impact levels.
    - **Select:** Identify and tailor security controls.
    - **Implement:** Apply the security controls to the system.
    - **Assess:** Evaluate the effectiveness of the controls.
    - **Authorize:** Grant permission to operate the system based on risk acceptance.
    - **Monitor:** Continuously oversee the system and respond to changes.
2. **Focus:**
    
    - Emphasizes continuous monitoring and integration with the system development lifecycle (SDLC).

### Application:

- Primarily used in U.S. federal agencies.
- Can be adapted for private-sector organizations.

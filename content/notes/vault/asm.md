- ***Attack Surface Management***

Discovering, managing and reducing attack surface of an organization. 
Included external and internal assets.

- Continuous monitoring of the attack surface.
- Identification of shadow IT (unauthorized or untracked assets).
- Risk prioritization and mitigation recommendations.

## **Benefits**:
- Improved visibility of the organization’s digital footprint.
- Proactive threat reduction.
- Better alignment with zero-trust security models.

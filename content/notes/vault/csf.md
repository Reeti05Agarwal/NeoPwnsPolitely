## **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)**

### Overview:

The **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)** is a flexible, voluntary framework developed by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) to manage and mitigate cybersecurity risks. It provides best practices, guidelines, and standards to enhance an organization’s cybersecurity posture.

### Components:

1. **Core:** Five primary functions:
    
    - **Identify:** Understand organizational risks, including assets, people, and systems.
    - **Protect:** Implement safeguards to limit the impact of potential incidents.
    - **Detect:** Develop mechanisms to identify cybersecurity events promptly.
    - **Respond:** Plan and execute activities during an incident.
    - **Recover:** Restore normal operations and mitigate future risks.
2. **Implementation Tiers:** Maturity levels that describe how well an organization aligns its cybersecurity program with the framework.
    
3. **Profiles:** Tailored alignment of the framework to specific organizational needs.
    

### Application:

- Suitable for industries of all sizes.
- Commonly used in critical infrastructure sectors.

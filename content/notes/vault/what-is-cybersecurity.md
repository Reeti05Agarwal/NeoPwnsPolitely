“Cybersecurity is essentially about making sure the systems we rely on every day—like banking apps, healthcare systems, or even traffic control—can’t be tampered with or abused. The goal is to protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data.”

- **Human layer** – phishing, social engineering, weak passwords.
    
- **Network layer** – firewalls, IDS/IPS, packet inspection.
    
- **Application layer** – secure coding, input validation, patching.
    
- **Data layer** – encryption, access control, data classification.


“If an attacker intercepts traffic on a Wi-Fi network, protocols like TLS ensure the data is useless without the decryption keys.”


- _“Malware can hide in memory (fileless attacks), so defenders use behavioral analysis rather than just signature-based detection.”_
    
- _“Privilege escalation exploits system misconfigurations; hardening and least privilege policies help prevent that.”_

“It’s not just about firewalls anymore. We’re moving toward AI-driven threat detection, Zero Trust models, and resilience against things like ransomware-as-a-service. Cybersecurity is less about building walls and more about adapting to a constantly changing battlefield.”
